How to read

Observed reference: the temperature the station measured around the target hour. It is the average of its 15-minute readings at the hour and 15 minutes later. If only one of the two exists, that one is used (see Observation method). If neither exists there is no reference, and the error is unavailable, never 0.

J0 is not an observation: it is the forecast model's most recent run for that hour, shown as a reference. J1 to J7 are forecasts made 1 to 7 days ahead.

Error = forecast − observed reference. +2 °C means the forecast was 2 °C too warm.
